# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return Window

Most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to the original
payment method. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all tags and
accessories. Final-sale items (marked as such on the product page) and gift cards cannot be
returned.

## How to Start a Return

Customers can start a return from the "Order History" page in their account by selecting the
order and clicking "Return Item." This generates a prepaid return shipping label for domestic
orders. International returns require the customer to cover return shipping unless the item
arrived defective.

## Refund Timing

Once we receive a returned item, it is inspected within 2 business days. Refunds are issued to
the original payment method and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on the
customer's bank or card issuer. We do not offer cash or check refunds under any circumstance.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange an item for a different size or color, return
the original item for a refund and place a new order. This ensures the replacement ships
immediately rather than waiting on the return to be received first.

## Refunds for Orders Paid Partly with Store Credit

If an order was paid partly with store credit and partly with a card, the refund is applied to
store credit first, up to the amount originally used, with the remainder refunded to the card.

## Defective or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives defective, damaged, or is not what was ordered, contact support within 14
days of delivery. We will cover return shipping in full and offer either a replacement or a
full refund, whichever the customer prefers. Defective-item refunds do not require the 30-day
return window and can be processed even if the item has been used.

## When a Human Should Handle It

Refund requests involving amounts over $200, suspected fraud, or a customer disputing a charge
with their bank (a chargeback) should always be escalated to a human agent rather than
processed automatically.
