# Account FAQ

## Creating an Account

An account is created automatically the first time you check out, using the email address you
provide. You can also create one directly from the "Sign Up" page without placing an order.
Accounts are free and there is no requirement to keep a card on file.

## Resetting Your Password

Click "Forgot Password" on the sign-in page and enter your account email. A reset link is sent
immediately and expires after 60 minutes. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes,
check spam and confirm the email matches the one on the account — we do not tell customers
whether an email exists in our system, for security reasons.

## Changing Your Email Address

Go to Account Settings → Profile and enter the new email. A confirmation link is sent to the
*new* address; the change does not take effect until that link is clicked. This protects
against someone else changing your email if your account is compromised.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

2FA can be enabled under Account Settings → Security. We support authenticator apps (TOTP) and
SMS codes. We strongly recommend authenticator apps, since SMS can be intercepted via SIM-swap
attacks. Losing access to your 2FA method requires identity verification with our security
team before it can be removed — this cannot be done through the normal support flow.

## Closing an Account

Accounts can be closed from Account Settings → Close Account. This is permanent: order history,
saved addresses, and store credit are deleted and cannot be recovered. Any unused store credit
is forfeited on closure. We recommend using remaining store credit before closing an account.

## Account Security Escalations

Any request involving a compromised account, an unauthorized login, or a customer who says they
did not make changes appearing on their account must be escalated to a human agent immediately.
Do not attempt to verify identity or make account changes in these cases — this is a security
matter, not a routine support request.
