# Privacy & Data Security

## What Data We Collect

We collect account information (name, email, billing address), usage data (features used,
login timestamps), and content you create in the product. We do not sell customer data to
third parties, and we do not use your content to train third-party AI models without explicit
opt-in consent.

## Data Retention

Account data is retained for as long as the account is active. If an account is closed, data
is deleted within 30 days, except where we're required to retain billing records for tax and
legal purposes (typically 7 years for financial records, kept separately from product data).

## Data Export

Customers can export their data at any time from Account Settings → Export Data. Exports are
provided as a downloadable ZIP file containing JSON and CSV files and are generated within a
few minutes for most accounts; very large accounts may take up to 24 hours.

## Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Passwords are hashed and never
stored in plaintext or logged.

## Compliance

We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant for customers in the EU/UK. A signed Data
Processing Agreement (DPA) is available for Business and Enterprise customers on request via
the account's dedicated account manager or sales contact.

## Reporting a Security Issue

Suspected vulnerabilities should be reported to our security team rather than general support.
Do not attempt to test or exploit a suspected vulnerability against production systems or
another customer's account. Any report of a customer's account being compromised, or of data
belonging to one customer being visible to another, must be escalated to a human agent
immediately — this is never something to resolve through standard troubleshooting steps.
