# Pricing & Plans

## Plan Overview

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Free | Trying the product, personal use |
| Pro | $19/month or $190/year | Individuals and small teams |
| Business | $49/user/month | Growing teams that need admin controls |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing | Large organizations with compliance needs |

Annual billing saves approximately 17% compared to paying monthly (two months free).

## What's Included in Each Plan

- **Starter** — core features, up to 3 projects, community support only.
- **Pro** — unlimited projects, priority email support, advanced integrations.
- **Business** — everything in Pro, plus SSO, role-based permissions, and a dedicated account
  manager for accounts with 10+ seats.
- **Enterprise** — everything in Business, plus a custom contract, SLA-backed uptime guarantee,
  and audit logging. Enterprise pricing is negotiated directly with sales and is not published.

## Changing Plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated — you're charged only the difference for the
remaining days in your billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing
cycle, so you keep your current plan's features until then. You cannot downgrade below your
current seat count without first removing users.

## Billing Cycle and Invoices

Monthly plans bill on the same day each month as the original signup date. Annual plans bill
once per year on the signup anniversary. Invoices are available under Billing → Invoice History
and are also emailed automatically at the time of each charge.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails, we retry automatically three times over 7 days. If all retries fail, the
account is downgraded to the Starter plan and the account owner is notified by email. No data
is deleted when this happens — upgrading again restores full access immediately.

## Refunds on Subscriptions

Subscription charges are refundable within 14 days of the charge date if the plan was not used
meaningfully (e.g., no projects created, no team members invited). Refund requests after 14
days, or disputes about whether a plan was "used," should be escalated to a human agent.
