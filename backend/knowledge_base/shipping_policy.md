# Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping (United States)

We offer three shipping speeds for orders within the United States:

- **Standard Shipping** — 5–7 business days, $4.99 (free on orders over $50)
- **Expedited Shipping** — 2–3 business days, $12.99
- **Overnight Shipping** — 1 business day, $29.99 (order must be placed before 1:00 PM ET)

Orders are processed within 1 business day of being placed. Processing time is separate from
transit time — a Standard order placed on Monday typically ships Tuesday and arrives the
following Monday or Tuesday.

## International Shipping

We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. International orders
take 7–14 business days and are charged a flat rate of $19.99. Customers are responsible for
any customs duties or import taxes assessed by their country; these are not included in the
shipping charge and are not refundable if a package is refused at customs.

## Order Tracking

Every order receives a tracking number by email within 24 hours of shipment. Tracking links
route to the carrier's site (UPS, FedEx, or USPS depending on destination). If tracking shows
no movement for more than 5 business days, contact support and reference your order number so
we can open a carrier trace.

## Lost or Damaged Packages

If a package is marked delivered but never arrived, wait 48 hours (carriers sometimes scan
early) and then contact support. We will file a claim with the carrier and, once the claim is
confirmed, either reship the order at no cost or issue a full refund — your choice. Damaged
items should be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage and packaging;
we cannot process damage claims reported after that window.

## Address Changes

We can change a shipping address only if the order has not yet entered "processing" status.
Once a label has been printed, the address is locked and the order must be intercepted with
the carrier directly, which we are not always able to guarantee.
